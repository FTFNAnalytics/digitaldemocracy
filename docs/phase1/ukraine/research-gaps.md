## UA-BD-G07 — Mayor and other executive selection modes

For 2020, cities below 75,000 voters use relative majority; cities with at least 75,000 voters use absolute majority and conditional runoff. The threshold counts voters, not residents.

**Hold:** Oblast/raion/city-district council chairs, council secretaries, appointed administrations and Prime Minister are not additional popular executive offices. Starostas are council-approved under current Art. 54-1; separately elected historical starostas shown in the 2015 archive remain untranscribed history, not invented current offices. The 2010 book describes the older relative-majority head-election rule.
