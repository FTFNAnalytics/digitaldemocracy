# Malta research gaps and named gates

## Coverage delivered

The current register covers 68 local councils, their 68 mayor and 68 deputy-mayor offices, six indirectly elected regional presidencies, the House, the indirectly elected President and Malta's EP delegation. Two historical offices retain the Gozo Civic Council and its presidency. This is an office register, not a live list of all incumbents.

Council returns cover all 68 councils in 2024 and 2019, plus 34 in 2015 and 34 in 2013: three ordinary returns per council. National district returns cover 2003, 2008, 2013, 2017, 2022 and 2026. EP candidate/count returns cover 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024. Additional parliamentary declarations in 2026 and selected indirect elections are retained separately.

## Open gates

- **Presidential indirect election:** 2019 and 2024 resolutions are documented. Numeric House division tallies and the full earlier presidential-resolution series remain uncollected. A unanimous description is not converted into an invented vote count. Current article 48 requires two thirds; the 2020 amendment is not projected backwards.
- **STV transfers:** all count groups exposed by the selected ordinary-return pages were captured. Published count tallies and signed deltas are retained, including ellipses; underlying paper-level transfers/recounts are not invented. The pack does not claim independent recertification or a reconstruction of ballot papers.
- **Post-election changes:** source elected displays mix original winners and subsequent returns. Explicit election-count markers resolve ambiguous original winners; unannotated later entries remain source observations. Casual-election count tables, co-option instruments and legal/court adjustments are not exhaustively normalized. Some historical portal date annotations appear inconsistent (notably 2003-era pages); preserve them verbatim and require corroboration before creating a dated proceeding.
- **Mayoral selection:** article 25 and the Seventh Schedule establish the conditional first-preference rule and council-vote fallback. Council-by-council first meetings, refusals, tie rules, no-confidence replacements and current incumbencies are not exhaustively audited. No separate popular mayor/deputy ballot is asserted.
- **Local creation and boundaries:** 1993 founding 67 and Mtarfa's December 1999 addition are source-established gates. Earlier elections (1993–2012), historic boundary amendments and exact effective territorial versions require further work. No unverified local merger or successor links are inserted. Zero historical local-council rows means no separately discontinued local council was established here; it is not proof that boundaries never changed.
- **Regional bodies:** six current regional presidencies have indirect electoral footing. Two contested 2021 regional-list outcomes are retained. Four other 2021 sole nominees are recorded as nominations, not silently converted into certified wins. Appointed regional membership/deputies are excluded. The Act's six regions and older five-committee wording in S.L.363.160 require legal version care; the current Act and official current territorial register govern the denominator. No old/new regional successor map is guessed.
- **Gozo Civic Council:** National Archives confirms establishment, election on 4 June 1961 and council election of Anton Tabone on 4 July. Candidate votes, remaining cycles and original dissolution instrument are outstanding. No successor link to the modern Gozo Region is claimed.
- **European Parliament:** five ordinary returns are captured. Original 2004/2009 five-seat elections must not be overwritten with the current six-seat entitlement. EP 2024 corroboration distinguishes final vote shares from constitutive-session seat figures. Subsequent MEP replacements and Malta's later sixth-seat transition are not reconstructed as a new popular election.
- **Certified versus preliminary:** EC official-result publication status is retained; independent Government Gazette certification is not asserted for every row. EP's final/constitutive labels are preserved. No preliminary projection is promoted to certification.
- **Exact inherited field contract:** 223 table-column pairs are retained from the prior Atlas contract. A new production-schema pin was not checked. Mapping/tier acceptance remains for Justin; no operational execution occurs.

All gaps are about missing evidence or bounded historical coverage. Missing is never replaced with zero. All current offices survive regardless of the alert window.
