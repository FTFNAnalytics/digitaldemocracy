# North Macedonia research gaps and resolved gates

This is a complete current register with partial historic numeric coverage. Resolved scope questions remain here so reviewers can distinguish them from missing evidence.

| ID | Status | Finding / remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| presidential-runoff | open | Current first-round threshold is majority of all registered voters; runoff requires the legal turnout threshold, currently 40%. Separate source events retained. Do not back-apply the current threshold to 1994, 1999 or 2004. Older statutory editions and full numeric vectors remain to be transcribed. |
| mayor-selection-mode | resolved | All 80 municipal mayors and the City of Skopje mayor are popularly elected. Four-year terms, first-round majority of votes cast and one-third turnout; top-two runoff. No council-elected mayor offices invented. Primary consolidated code byte-level review remains a separate legal evidence gap. |
| skopje-nesting | resolved | Ten component municipalities each have a council and mayor; City of Skopje separately has a council and mayor. Geography nesting is not office succession. City council has 45 seats by law. |
| municipal-reforms-2004 | open | The 2004 change from 123 to 84 municipalities is a named gate. All abolished/altered pre-2004 units and exact legal commencement provisions have not been inventoried. No guessed offices, dates or merger edges are produced. |
| municipal-reforms-2013 | partly_resolved | ODIHR2013 p.4 footnote5 names Kichevo, Zajas, Oslomej, Vraneshtica and Drugovo; the change takes effect from the 2013 local elections. Four former units produce eight historical-only offices. No successor-office relation is inferred. Historical numeric local results remain incomplete. |
| name-change-2019 | resolved | 12 February 2019 Prespa entry into force; Republic of Macedonia / international provisional reference FYROM to Republic of North Macedonia. Same independent-republic office identities; no duplicate offices or invented election. |
| no-ep | resolved | EU candidate, not Member State: zero European Parliament offices, events and results. EP observer delegations are not domestic elected offices. |
| independence-and-1991-president | resolved | 1990 Yugoslav constituent-republic election is excluded. The 1991 president was elected by the Assembly; no invented popular 1991 presidential contest. The first direct presidential election is 1994. |
