# Albania identity rules — BA addendum

The unchanged prior identity contract is in `reference/Prior_Albania_Identity_Rules.md`. The following research additions do not authorize deployment or bind a production release.

1. Preserve all 122 existing local office IDs. AL-13-M is Belsh; AL-14-M is Cërrik. Do not renumber them by INSTAT classification order. Country key remains `albania`; namespace reference `cdd-observatory-v1`; lineage reference `country-package-albania`.
2. The inherited geography key uses the ordered vector `["albania", original Jurisdiction, original Office]`. Office type participates. Retain the recovered geography IDs rather than silently consolidating mayor/council geography keys.
3. An existing history key is `OfficeID::year::YYYY-MM-DD`. Event key is `event-` plus the first 24 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 of its compact UTF-8 JSON vector `["albania", history_key]`. No date reformatting or label normalization is applied before hashing.
4. Existing result IDs are the event ID plus `-r` and the zero-based recovered row index. Inherited workbook vote/seat vectors and order were compared to the briefing. This is a recovered research binding; production shard rows are not independently certified here.
5. New national identity `AL-NAT-ASSEMBLY` is a draft research allocation for the post-1991 Republic body, not a duplicate People's Assembly. Constitutional transition history is held separately. An election before the April 1991 transition is not automatically mapped to this office.
6. Historical IDs have form `AL-H2011-<12 uppercase hex>-M/C`. Hash input is the compact JSON tuple of normalized qark, district and complete 2011 unit label. Normalization removes spaces/diacritics/punctuation for key comparison, while original labels remain stored. One explicit abbreviation expansion joins KOMUNA Q MALESI E MADHE to KOMUNA QENDER MALESI E MADHE. No fuzzy or same-name merger is allowed.
7. These historical identities denote a sourced 2011 territorial vintage. They do not assert all units were abolished. Any continuity to post-reform municipalities remains unresolved. `office_successor_edges=[]` is deliberate. The reform's general 2015 transition does not supply each office's exact end date.
8. The 11 2011 Tirana boroughs each have a separately elected head and council. Their parent references identify the historical Tirana city pair, not succession. Current administrative units are not assigned these historical offices.
9. Ura Vajgurore → Dimal is the same-unit rename under Law 34/2021. Preserve AL-05-M/C. Adoption 16 March and promulgation 1 April 2021 are not substituted for an untranscribed effective day. INSTAT's stale old name is recorded alongside current Dimal, not counted as another municipality.
10. A council and direct executive are distinct elected bodies, each with its own row and draft tier. A mandate, member, candidate or party is not a separate office row.
11. Polling phases, cancelled scheduled polls, annulled counts, repeat polls and final cycle allocations have distinct meanings. A year-only event uses the event-kind token in its history key; it does not use a fake 1 January. Early national cycle summaries use range metadata and are not extra polls.
12. For Rrogozhinë, May and July 2023 have different event IDs. July explicitly repeats May; this is a sourced event relation, not an office successor edge. The May archive vector is recounted and annulled. The 1 August certificate is a decision date, not the July polling date.
13. The planned Tirana 9 November 2025 event keeps that date as a scheduled date, marks the poll as cancelled and has no results. Court suspension/announcement dates remain separate fields. No replacement event is guessed.
14. Party and coalition labels are event-local. Do not merge coalitions by name similarity or turn Others/independents aggregates into stable party identities. No cross-cycle mapping or swing score is supplied.
15. Null means unknown, untranscribed, inapplicable or unresolved as labelled. Zero is retained only as explicitly reported or an inherited recorded zero with its qualification. Positive 2011 seat cells are transcribed; blank cells do not become zero. Winner-only rows do not get a fabricated one seat or vote count.
16. Preliminary, certified, dashboard and secondary-report evidence are separate states. Finality of an election process or winner does not prove every numeric dashboard cell. A source being an official observer's final report does not make a copied preliminary return final.
17. Expected cycles and exact dates differ. Next polling dates are null unless sourced. The alert window never controls office/history existence. No popular President, qark, prefect, PM or EP office is introduced.
18. Draft tier rows are exactly 1:1 with all 891 office rows, including historical ones. Research display tier national maps to the inherited `national_context` enum only in the field-map proposal. No classifications are approved or applied.
