## Binding and null policy

All source references resolve through S.input_path and byte hash. For JSON use RFC6901 pointers, for XLSX exact sheet/row/column, for PDF page/article, and for HTML exact XPath or article/text anchor. Evidence objects retain those source locators. A known resolved source absent from S is a fatal FK error, not an unresolved token. Authored research-gap tokens are separately labelled and bind real geography records.

NULL means not supplied/not resolved/withheld as labelled; 0 is only an explicit numeric zero. '*', '-', blanks and sex/category not-applicable markers are retained without inferred zero. Numeric conflicting observations keep all claims and withhold only the contested scalar; no certified-total or alternate-share repair. Optional date components remain NULL. All raw source fields, unknown headers, law wording, footnotes, sex tables and incomplete coverage notes survive exact retained bytes and raw_json.

## Dataset overlap and retention

Full raw XLSX sources are retained. Sparse documentary projections do not replace originals. Federal CH and constituency aggregates, States party and candidate representations, and repeated rolling-year snapshots must not be added as independent cycles. City rows with no explicit Wahljahr remain snapshots. Government composition is retained separately from the reported election-year anchor. First/second States rounds use Q; no invented cycle from each candidate or numeric result column. Evidence of a citizen assembly does not generate an elected legislature.

The office universe is incomplete, independently of mapping coverage. All municipality gaps are enumerated in A=`.../commune-coverage-audit.json`; future ingestion must not translate missing institutional evidence into nonexistent offices. Justin accepted the evidenced subset; the 308 commune-executive gaps, thin historic/merger archive, 1,938 parliament caveats, and disputed-result notes stay open. There are real regional bodies but no generated upcoming calendar events.
