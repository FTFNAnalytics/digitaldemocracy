The event count is office-level and component-level, not a count of nationwide election days. Historical event coverage and result transcription are separate measures. Ordinary local series: 64 councils in 2010; 71 councils and 71 popular executives in 2014; 64 councils and 64 mayors in 2017, 2021 and 2025. One Tbilisi popular mayor event is retained for 2010. The five continuation councils have separate year-precision 2006 origin records.

The CEC historical compendium supplies older national/Adjara figures, but not every table has been transcribed. The 1995 and 1999 parliamentary vectors are explicitly partial. The 2003 PR event is annulled and has no numeric result rows. The 2004 repeat is separate. The 2020 national workbook contains blanks: 23 independently published ODIHR totals are retained, while the other 27 party totals remain null pending reconciliation; no aggregate “27 parties” total is assigned to any one list. Untranscribed majoritarian districts, early local tiers and by-elections remain holds.

2021 council seat rows are an independently sourced final vector for all 64 councils, across both PR and district components. They are separate result rows with votes null and `result_kind=whole_council_final_seat_allocation`; they are not additional votes or a second council contest.

The referenced CEC compendium and retained 2014 workbooks include runoff evidence, but the 29 June repeat-poll local-office mapping is not completed. It is retained as an unassigned observation, not falsely linked to Parliament or a guessed municipality. Preliminary 2001 Adjara head/Senate evidence is not upgraded to final certification. Earlier pre-independence material in the source documents does not create normalized office/event rows.
