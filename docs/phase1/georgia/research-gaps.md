## GE-BG-G03 — Presidential mode and 1992 interval

Popular presidential contests are retained for 1991 and 1995–2018. The 1992 popular Chair of Parliament/head-of-state ballot is a distinct historical office. The 2018 direct-election rule required more than 50% of valid votes; otherwise the top two proceeded to a runoff two weeks after official announcement of first-round results (ODIHR 2018 section IV). That rule is not projected into the indirect era. Since the 2024 transition, a 300-member Electoral College elects the President; Parliament is only one component.

Next evidence needed: Do not create a current popular presidential office or a popular 2029 presidential event.
