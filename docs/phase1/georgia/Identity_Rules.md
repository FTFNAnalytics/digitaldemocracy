# Georgia BG — identity rules

1. Namespace `georgia_BG_research`, country `georgia` / `GE`; IDs are pack-local research keys, not existing Atlas database IDs. Never use US-GA.
2. One office per institution and evidenced electoral geography/era. A legislature/council row is the body, not one row per member or district seat. Direct executives have distinct rows.
3. Current normal municipalities: 64, independently reconciled between the 2025 returns and Geostat. Source election codes are not cadastral IDs. Tbilisi DECs 1–10 map to one municipality; its council and mayor remain two distinct offices.
4. The five article-164 Georgian councils are current statutory-continuation scope holds. Their 2006-origin events have year precision until locality protocols are matched. Council-selected gamgebeli do not generate popular offices, dates or votes.
5. Seven reform areas have pre-2014 council geography rows and separate 2014 city/community council and popular-executive rows. Current consolidated bodies have post-2017 geography. All predecessor/successor fields remain null. A source name or recycled district number is not a merger edge.
6. National President means only the evidenced popular-election eras in the office register. The 1992 popular Chair/head-of-state office is explicitly separate. The current indirect President is calendar context, not a current popular office. No duplicated current president.
7. The historical Adjara Supreme Council, 2001 Republican Council, Senate and popular head are source-identified. No current Senate or popular regional head is inferred. The national Constitution's contingent upper-house provision is not an elected national chamber.
8. An event is an office/date/ballot-component/round record. The same day may contain distinct repeat and runoff proceedings in different districts. An annulled component has its own outcome. Partial-district votes cannot be silently combined with a full national or municipal party vector.
9. Published party/list labels and ballot numbers are election-context labels; no enduring party merger, alliance or successor mapping is asserted. “Others” in a seat table is a source aggregate, not a political party.
10. JSON null means missing, untranscribed, withheld or not applicable, with the applicable status text. Zero is retained only when a source supplies a numeric zero in a participating result context; null is never replaced to force an arithmetic identity.
11. Result `votes` are direct published aggregates or sums of evidenced numeric precinct cells. Any missing constituent cell makes an aggregate null; `known_partial_vote_sum` is an audit value, not an official total. An independently published final aggregate may fill the result without changing blank source cells.
12. District subtotal rows are excluded from precinct aggregation. Uniform zero/absent columns alone do not establish local party participation or runoff candidacy. Candidate names are not guessed from ballot numbers.
13. PR votes, PR seats and whole-council final seat allocations have different result kinds. The 2021 complete council seat vector includes both rounds and must not be summed again with PR-only seats. The national 2016/2020 conflicting total-seat columns remain held.
14. Evidence stages remain explicit: signed SEC summary, official historical compendium citing a protocol, ODIHR-reproduced final figures, preliminary account, or mirrored CEC-described workbook. A source filename never establishes certification.
15. Occupation/control is a scope hold, not a second country identity. No parallel Abkhaz/South Ossetian institution is normalized. No diplomatic conclusion or current popular vote is inferred from the Electoral College membership clause.
16. Only a law or official call establishes a future formula/date. Derived next years are labeled; exact future day fields stay null here. Historical offices get no upcoming events. Outside-window dates remain prominent country entries.
17. No EP, appointed cabinet/Prime Minister, popular mkhare governor, party organ or current nested Tbilisi district executive office is invented. Statistical regions and constituency boundaries are not automatically elected bodies.
18. `registry_qualified=false`, all approval flags false, `applied_changes=0`. The field map is documentary; it must not be treated as authorization to import or publish.
