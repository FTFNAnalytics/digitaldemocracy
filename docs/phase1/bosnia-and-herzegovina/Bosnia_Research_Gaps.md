# Bosnia and Herzegovina — Prompt AW research gaps

Current office scope is reconciled to the source-supported 2024/2025 structure. Open items below do not authorize fabrication.

## BA-AW-G01 — candidate/list numeric result vectors

**Status:** open

Contest coverage and official result/certification state are represented for every completed event, but this pack does not claim exhaustive candidate/list vote/share/seat transcription for all 918 completed event records. Missing numeric fields are NULL/not_transcribed, never zero.

## BA-AW-G02 — historical city-status legal effective dates

**Status:** open

Twenty CIK basic constituencies move from municipal to city category between 2016, 2020 and 2024 rosters. Historical office rows are retained, but no legal successor edge/effective date is asserted without the specific legal act.

## BA-AW-G03 — Republika Srpska vice-president stable identity

**Status:** open

There are two vice-president offices. Internal slots 01/02 avoid inventing permanent constituent-people seats; 2022 official selected pair is retained only at aggregate outcome level.

## BA-AW-G04 — House of Peoples / indirect chambers

**Status:** documented_gate

BiH House of Peoples, FBiH House of Peoples and RS Council of Peoples are documented as indirectly elected and excluded from the direct-popular office register.

## BA-AW-G05 — Brčko executive

**Status:** documented_gate

Brčko Assembly is included; Brčko mayor is council/assembly-selected and no direct executive row is created.

## BA-AW-G06 — Sarajevo / Mostar executives

**Status:** documented_gate

Sarajevo and Mostar mayors are council-selected; no direct-executive rows are created. Sarajevo City Council itself is retained as an indirect elected body.

## BA-AW-G07 — European Parliament

**Status:** documented_gate

Zero EP offices. Bosnia and Herzegovina is an EU candidate, not a member state.

## BA-AW-G08 — pre-2014 local territorial history

**Status:** open

Prompt AW provides three local cycles (2016/2020/2024) and three completed general cycles (2014/2018/2022). Older municipal boundary/successor history is not inferred and remains a future historical-depth task.

## BA-AW-G09 — 2024 direct-mayor aggregate count

**Status:** source_discrepancy

CEC 8 May 2024 English call page enumerates 111 municipal mayors + 31 city mayors but also states 143 total; the 4 October CEC basic-information page says 32 city mayors and 143 total. Prompt AW does not create an unidentified 143rd direct office. It retains 142 direct local executives identified by the direct-office roster and separately documents council-selected Sarajevo, Mostar and Brčko mayors.
