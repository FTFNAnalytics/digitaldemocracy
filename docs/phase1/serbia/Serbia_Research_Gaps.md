# Serbia research gaps — Prompt AX

## RS-AX-G01 — local_numeric_vectors
**Status:** open

The current office register and three official local contest observations per current local assembly are present, but exhaustive candidate/list vote/seat vectors for every 2016-series, 2020-series and latest local contest have not been transcribed. Numeric omissions are NULL/not_transcribed, never zero.

## RS-AX-G02 — presidential_runoff
**Status:** documented

President is directly elected. First-round majority rule and statutory second round are preserved; 2012 first and second rounds are separate events.

## RS-AX-G03 — vojvodina_executive
**Status:** documented

Vojvodina Assembly is an elected office. The Provincial Government is elected by the Assembly; no direct provincial executive office is created.

## RS-AX-G04 — mayors
**Status:** documented

Municipal presidents and city mayors are selected by elected assemblies under the local system represented by the SORS bulletins. No current direct local executive office is created, including Belgrade mayor.

## RS-AX-G05 — kosovo
**Status:** scope_gate

Kosovo municipal/parallel registers are excluded from this Serbia pack under the standing Atlas gate. SORS local-election bulletins also state Kosovo data are outside their totals. No dual-sovereignty rows are created.

## RS-AX-G06 — european_parliament
**Status:** documented

Serbia is not an EU Member State; zero European Parliament offices are created.

## RS-AX-G07 — historical_status_reforms
**Status:** open

Municipal/city status changes and any legal predecessor/successor graph require source-specific legal verification. No successor link is guessed; successor-crosswalk.json is empty.

## RS-AX-G08 — 2020_local_dates
**Status:** open

The SORS 2020 publication/result series is retained as a historical contest observation, but per-office poll dates are not transcribed. Event dates are explicitly unknown rather than assigned the national 2020 election day.

## RS-AX-G09 — source_bytes
**Status:** documented

Source files in sources/ are normalized research extracts created for this handoff. Their SHA-256 hashes authenticate those extracts, not the original remote web/PDF bytes.

## RS-AX-G10 — city_municipality_filter
**Status:** documented

The statistical register counts 28 city municipalities in the relevant Serbia structure, but election tables show no separate elected councillors for the central same-name Užice, Požarevac and Vranje statistical units. Sevojno, Kostolac and Vranjska Banja are included; the central statistical rows are not offices.
