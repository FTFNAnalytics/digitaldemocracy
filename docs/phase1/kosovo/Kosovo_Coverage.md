# Coverage and limitations

Current core: **77 offices**, one Assembly plus 38 municipal assemblies and 38 direct mayors. Historical: **61 source-vintage identities**, not 61 proven abolitions. Every office has an unapproved draft tier.

| Local cycle | Included / held |
|---|---|
| 2000 | 30 historic assembly events; 27 certified numeric vectors |
| 2002 | 30 historic assembly events and numeric vectors |
| 2007 | 30 historic assembly, 30 first-round mayor and 23 runoff vectors |
| 2009 | Calendar found, municipality-vintage and all result vectors remain held; not backfilled with today’s 38 municipalities |
| 2013 | 38 assembly and 38 first-round events; Zvecan vectors missing; 25 runoff vectors; phase repeats separated |
| 2017 | 38 assembly and 38 first-round vectors; 19 actual runoff territories plus Partesh first-round repeat; archive phase labels gated |
| 2021 | 38 assembly, 38 first-round and 21 runoff vectors; Dragash postal repeat gated |
| 2025 | 38 assembly, 38 first-round and 18 runoff events; 37 council seat reports; 20 first-round winners; 17 preliminary runoff party leaders |

National contests: 2001, 2004, 2007, 2010, 2014, 2017, 2019, 2021, February 2025, December 2025, June 2026. The 2010 cycle also has separate December/January poll records. 2004 contains ten named vote rows; 2007 partial votes and explicit mandates; 2014 represented lists; 2021 preserved votes with seats held; December/June recent contests represented-list seats/shares with most votes untranscribed. This is not an exhaustive all-candidate history.

Specials covered: North Mitrovica February 2014; four northern mayor replacements May 2019; Podujevë/North Mitrovica November 2020; four mayor/two assembly polls April 2023; four failed recalls April 2024. Their calendar-only records do not contain fabricated candidate results.

57 events have no transcribed result rows. Of 3489 results, 3213 have votes and 786 explicit seats. Keep result_coverage and evidence qualifiers; these totals do not imply full vectors per event.

Known discrepancies are recorded, not repaired: missing 2013 Zveçan; 2010 denominator; 2014 competing snapshots; uncertain repeat consolidation; 2026 source share conflict. 2017, 2019 and February 2025 national vectors reconcile to EU certified tables. Canonical event arithmetic is checked where coherent totals are available.

Official cycle certification does not automatically certify earlier numerical snapshots. Source classes distinguish administration, official observer reports, independent CEC-data preservation and secondary reporting. Nested locality councils, joint-board operative rules, 2009 and missing replacement census remain held. No future poll date is populated merely from term length.
