1. Atlas Kosovo jurisdiction scope only. Country ID kosovo; XK is an internal user-assigned code, not an ISO or diplomatic assertion. No dual-state office or copy into Serbia AX.
2. One unicameral constitutional Assembly. President selected by the Assembly; PM/government not separate popular offices. No presidential public election from an office-holder change.
3. One municipal assembly and one direct mayor per sourced current municipality. Research IDs XK-01–XK-38 align to the preserved archive lookup, not statutory numbering. EU annex ordinals differ and must not be used as archive codes.
4. Gllogoc/Gllogovc/Drenas are source-matched name variants. Mamushë/Hani i Elezit Serbian archive translations are swapped; use legal corroboration. No renamed-party or coalition-successor guess.
5. North and South Mitrovica are distinct current municipalities. Early undivided Mitrovica is a historical source unit. No additional popular joint board, second capital or two Presidents.
6. 2000/2002/2007 municipal bodies and 2001/2004/2007 provisional Assembly retain historical identities. Historical-only does not prove abolition or validity-end date. No pre-1999 office is imported into modern scope.
7. The 2008 Constitution, Law 03/L-041, staged municipal establishment and 2013 arrangements are gates. transition-relations is empty; spelling, numbers or territory similarity never justify a successor edge.
8. Poll, certification, announcement and publication dates differ. Repeat does not create an office. Separate first rounds, runoffs, annulments, replacements, postal subsets and recalls. Uncertain combined vectors are snapshots/aggregates without guessed dates.
9. Party-list ballots are not candidate preferences. Candidate identity requires explicit source metadata join. Cycle Elected flags do not prove round victory; parties are not offices.
10. Blank, missing, hyphen, inaccessible and untranscribed are not zero. Explicit zeros retain source_zero. Derived percentages state their denominator. Source conflicts stay null. Other aggregate is not a party.
11. No global party grouping or coalition/party successor map. Srpska Lista can be a contest label inside Kosovo elections without becoming a Serbia-scope institution.
12. Elected locality advisory councils are an open category census, not flattened into municipalities or invented. No generic region/prefecture tier is added.
13. One unapproved draft tier per office. Alert horizon never determines existence/history. No polling day from term arithmetic.
14. Hash original bytes and normalized fact bytes separately. Indexed-only text receives no invented original hash. All application writes and Justin approvals remain untouched.
