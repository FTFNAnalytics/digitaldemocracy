## Contract and notation

All **223 columns / 20 tables** in the authoritative two migrations are mapped below. `schema_migration` is migration-owned, not research data. DDL bytes are not altered or executed. `column-map.json` is the matching machine-readable specification. Table selectors identify the file/pointer for every field; `/i` is a zero-based JSON array index, original PDF pages/sheet rows are one-based. `C` = country.json, `O` = office-register.json row, `G` = geography.json row, `E` = events.json row, `Rr` = results.json row, `S` = sources.json row, `T` = tier file, `X` = identity-crosswalk.json, `K` = counts.json, `I` = Spain_Input_Inventory.json. C(x) in formulas denotes canonical serialization, distinguished from country object by parentheses.

Common FK rule: all master active rows carry their own `(L,R)` and join the selected publication member for Spain, never the latest receipt or another country's release. Complete identities and source locators are supplied in Spain_Identity_Vectors.json. Runtime-only rows are specified but **not emitted**. A source empty/null cannot authorize an invented row.

## Projection and retention

Retain every original PDF/HTML/XLS/XLSX and derived extraction by hash. Source downloads are inert bytes; do not execute HTML/scripts/macros. Municipal original summaries overlap correction publications; keep one event per office/date. A correction is not another ordinary cycle. `municipal-source-blocks.jsonl.gz` contains 27,394 original blocks; 6,866 unbound/submunicipal blocks and two non-identical duplicate bindings remain explicitly retained, not silently converted to municipal events. Original nationwide totals are used once, never added to their province subtotals. No party normalization, computed percentage, margin, winner, geometry or proceeding is invented.

Original source claims with later correction risk remain research rows with holds, not certified final results. Two duplicate-source events are disputed. The future importer must either require approved reconciled inputs/overrides or preserve disputed evidence and withhold consequential presentation; this pack does not supply production acceptance. Approved regional calendar coverage is unavailable from this draft, although 68 offices are proposed regional. All next dates remain unknown; no office is filtered out by the alert window.

## Unknown fields and future extensions

Every original column, PDF label, extraction hold, electoral mode, acquisition failure, bibliography link, current-name alias and historical register observation survives in complete raw envelopes or retained_input. Missing schema columns do not justify discarding data or inventing new DDL. PDF page/bbox/cell coordinates are evidence locators inside raw JSON. A name-only historical alias is not a merger crosswalk. National candidate-mark ballots are not normalized by list-vote denominators. Bound correction provenance must preserve the original scalar and explicit accepted replacement/withhold decision before any mutation.
