# France identity and mechanism rules

All keys are reviewable research keys. They are not production IDs and no importer is supplied.

1. One office row represents an elected body or direct executive, not each seat, councillor, candidate, polling bureau or legislative constituency. AN, Senate, President and EP delegation are four distinct current rows.
2. Current municipal identity starts from INSEE COG2026 TYPECOM=COM and the 83 real overseas communes. Delegated, associated and administrative arrondissement entries do not automatically create council offices. Six Meuse appointed commissions are excluded by name.
3. Current municipal keys are FR-MUN-{COG code}; code is a string. Leading zeroes and Corsican 2A/2B survive. Historical keys append an explicit before-date gate when a documented creation or code transition intervenes. This separates a pre-merger office even when a new commune retains its code. It does not assert the legal successor.
4. INSEE movement rows are geographic evidence, not office succession. There are no guessed edges in successor-crosswalk.json. Code-change continuity and legal successor approval remain open.
5. The Ministry's older ZA/ZB/ZC/ZD/ZM/ZN/ZP/ZS codes are mapped to their named territory. Three-digit commune suffixes embed an overseas prefix digit. The 2014 Mayotte 501–517 tokens are matched to COG97601–97617 and their source names. SR/SN suffixes are reporting sectors/sections of their parent municipal office, not autonomous communes. Original source bytes remain available.
6. Paris is one current municipal/departmental Council of Paris. Lyon municipal and metropolitan councils are separate elected bodies. The CEA is one departmental council across Bas-Rhin and Haut-Rhin; the administrative departments remain geography. Guyane, Martinique and Corsica each have one single territorial assembly.
7. Mayotte 976R is a 2026 territorial code. Do not apply its future 2028 list/52-seat system to the present transitional council or 2021 binomial election. No twin current office is created.
8. Fourteen ordinary regional councils coexist with three single territorial assemblies and transitional Mayotte. Historical 2010 regions remain separately identified when replaced; no guessed 2016 succession edges. Pre-2018 Corsica assembly remains historical-only.
9. PLM has 34 current sector/arrondissement councils evidenced by the 2026 dedicated returns. City and arrondissement ballots are separate in 2026. Older sector figures remain local reporting units of city council contests; they are not a reconstructed citywide total. Paris Centre is one council for the first four administrative arrondissements.
10. No popularly elected mayor, regional president, departmental president, overseas government president, prefect or Prime Minister is invented. Only the national President is a direct executive row. Council-selected executives are described on the relevant body row.
11. Senate electorate_type is electoral_college. PR and majority ballots are separate event variants, and majority rounds can occur on the same day. Multi-candidate marks cannot be summed as one ballot. AN is a separate direct popular chamber.
12. Presidential round 1 and round 2 are separate events with separate electorates, turnout and valid totals. The two-round system has no transfer papers or vote-conservation calculation between rounds.
13. Event identity combines office, sourced cycle year, round and where necessary Senate ballot variant. A reporting unit is nested within one event. Results are source-order rows within that unit; labels alone never identify an office or party.
14. Result grain remains explicit: 2014 municipality/bureau candidate-or-list entries; 2020 small-commune candidate marks; later list ballots; departmental binomials; indirect Senate votes; national percentages/seat compositions; returned representatives. Never sum across different grains.
15. NULL means not supplied or not safely attributable. Zero is retained only if a numeric zero is supplied. Empty elected marker is NULL. An elected representative without vote totals has votes=NULL. No percentages are converted into counts.
16. EP published national seat tables are constitutive-session compositions. In 2014 the official party composition may differ from election-night labels; in 2019 74 seated members is not the later 79. 2024 has 81. Partial Interior regional vote returns are not a national total.
17. Raw source status survives: publication under appeal/correction reservation, definitive-labelled presidential results, proclaimed COM results, and WF Sigave original return with repeat-election gate. Current office is not current incumbent.
18. Exact ordinary future dates are not inferred from six-year terms. Date precision is day/month/year; the alert window applies only to calendar screening. It never removes current offices or history.
19. Two 2020 list labels contain an unescaped tab. Their documented repair joins the label cells while preserving numeric source cells. The 2015 regional round-two header includes a stale composition column; its observed nine-cell list blocks are documented. These repairs are extraction rules, not source-number corrections.
20. Every office, including historical-only offices, has exactly one draft tier. Justin approval remains false. No source text can approve the pack.
