# Electoral mechanisms and coverage

| Class | Footing and treatment |
|---|---|
| House of Commons | UK-wide chamber; single-member constituency plurality/FPTP. Constituencies are reporting units. No alternative-vote or PR counterfactual returns. |
| Scottish Parliament | AMS: 73 constituency and 56 regional-list members. Separate ballots and regional allocation geography. First Minister is not a separate popular executive. |
| Senedd | 2026 closed-list PR: 16 constituencies, six members each, 96 total. Earlier 60-seat AMS vintages are separate. No invented list allocation mathematics. |
| Northern Ireland Assembly | STV in 18 constituencies; 90 seats since 2017, 108 in earlier retained cycles. First preferences do not equal final transfer totals. Executive selection is not a separate popular presidential vote. |
| London Assembly | AMS: 14 constituency members plus 11 London-wide members; separate constituency and list votes. |
| Mayor of London | Separate direct office; supplementary vote historically, FPTP in 2024, 2026 restoration of SV documented with law-vintage gate. |
| Strategic/combined-authority mayors | 13 current offices named in the register; elected mayor distinct from indirectly composed authority structures. Policing functions are attached to the same mayor office where transferred. |
| Local-authority executive mayors | 13 current offices. Ordinary civic mayors and council leaders are not automatically direct executives. All 13 have a sourced normalized return. |
| PCC/PFCC | 37 current standalone popular offices in England/Wales. No duplicates where policing is integrated into a mayoralty; no Scottish/NI PCC invention. FPTP 2024 and current SV legal vintage remain distinct. |
| English principal councils | County, district, unitary, metropolitan and London-borough classes retained. Single/multi-member ward plurality. County/district overlaps are different elected bodies. |
| City of London | Special resident/business ward electorate. Common Council and separately elected aldermanic component are distinguished; overlapping membership is not a second seat total. Lord Mayor is excluded as a direct popular executive. |
| Isles of Scilly | Special principal council, 16 members. 2025 sole contested island return plus 15 explicitly unopposed returns. The council chair is not a separately popular mayor. |
| Scottish councils | 32 councils; STV. 2022 aggregate first preferences/seats retained. No synthetic transfer papers. |
| Welsh principal councils | 22 county/county-borough councils; plurality election evidence retained. No nationwide directly elected mayor layer invented. |
| NI local councils | 11 councils, STV by district electoral area. First-preference extraction and full retained count-sheet evidence are separate coverage levels. |
| Parish/community/town | Source-identified subset only. Parish meetings, grouped councils, precept areas and actual elected councils cannot be equated automatically. Civic chairs/mayors are council selected. |
| Scottish national parks / Crofting Commission | Only elected components of mixed bodies are included, with distinct restricted franchises. Appointed members and board-selected chairs are excluded. |
| European Parliament | Historical UK delegation, 1979–2019 elections. GB plurality before 1999, regional lists thereafter; NI STV. No current UK EP office after withdrawal. |

Primary evidence and versions are linked in the source inventory and research gaps. Election mechanisms are not a licence to reconstruct missing votes, quota calculations or seat allocations. Current-law labels must not overwrite an older event's actual electoral system.
