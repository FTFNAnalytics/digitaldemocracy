## Selection modes and gates

The President is popularly elected with a conditional second round. Article 29 vacancy selection by Congress near the end of a term is an exceptional succession path, not a new ordinary indirect presidential office. Cámara and Senado are distinct chambers; Senate renewal is staggered. Governors became popularly elected in 2021; appointed intendentes and presidential delegates are excluded. Direct CORE elections began in 2013; Ñuble is not backfilled into the 2013/2017 direct-election roster. Modern mayor ballots are separate from council ballots from 2004; 1992–2000 combined municipal contests do not create invented separate mayor ballots.

Chile has zero EP offices, no invented provincial elected tier, no party-organ offices and no invented regional-parliament offices. The 2021 Convention and 2023 Constitutional Council are historical extraordinary bodies, with no guessed succession edges to Congress.

The principal outstanding work is transcription of 2024 municipal/CORE/first-round governor results and 2021 CORE results; resolution of historic spelling aliases and detailed repeats; and peer-policy confirmation for formal primaries. These holds are explicit and do not delete the current offices or their next ordinary dates.
