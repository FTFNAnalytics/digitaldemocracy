# Identity and continuity rules — Chile BJ

1. Preserve the existing country key `chile`, ISO code `CL`, and existing Atlas polity-kind/status identity. This pack supplies no diplomatic or sovereignty reclassification. Main was read at `702ac3a0717ab822b7d619e6e26ab012f499ff6e`; no branch, commit or importer was changed.
2. IDs in this pack are proposed documentary IDs, not claims about minted production keys. There was no prior accepted Chile office register to preserve. An empty stub produces **zero** predecessor/successor edges.
3. An office row denotes one elected institution, not every individual seat. Cámara and Senado are separate bodies. Electoral districts and Senate constituencies are result dimensions, not extra office rows. CORE provincial constituencies are voting divisions, not a popular provincial tier.
4. The SERVEL August2026 territorial workbook lists 346 comunas. The SUBDERE CUT workbook supplies official codes and region/province context. Exact unique names are crosschecked after case/diacritic/punctuation normalization; substantive spelling variants remain recorded holds. Current SERVEL names independently establish the two offices even when a CUT crosswalk is unresolved.
5. Antártica is explicitly grouped with Cabo de Hornos by Law19726, arts1–2. Both comunas point to the same mayor/council pair. The statute also identifies Navarino’s rename to Cabo de Hornos; this is a name/status continuity fact, not an invented merger. The grouping is not a sovereignty conclusion about Antarctica.
6. Other suspected aliases, creations, boundary transfers and mergers require direct evidence. No fuzzy name match becomes a successor edge. Historical geography labels remain in source facts; current Ñuble membership is not retrojected into an earlier electoral region.
7. One ordinary multi-day poll is one date-range event. Runoffs are separate events. Annulled/partially annulled originals and repeats remain separate. San Ramón’s TER final dataset is isolated from the May original and July repeat until its retained/repeated mesas are reconciled.
8. Votes are numbers only when the source supplies a number or the pack expressly aggregates identified components. Missing vote components make the aggregate vote field null, with the known partial sum separately recorded. A source zero remains zero. Shares are preserved as fractions where the workbook uses fractions; no missing numerator is reconstructed from a percentage. Seats remain null without explicit evidence.
9. `SI`, elected-sheet inclusion or a specific `ELECTO/ELECTA/*` marker can support a candidate’s elected status. Blank winner cells remain unknown, not false. National chamber size is not copied into every event or result row. Reserved/alternate candidate text is retained, not turned into an invented separate indigenous parliament.
10. SERVEL historical tables, TER-certified local files, TRICEL-certified summaries and preliminary returns are distinct evidence grades. The pack uses no 2025 preliminary returns in accepted result observations. Raw labels, sheet rows and aggregation predicates remain available.
11. List totals, valid votes, null ballots, blank ballots, registered electors and candidate votes are different observations. Subtotals and candidate rows must not be added together. The derived 2025 presidential national rows point to their certified territorial components and must not be added to those components again.
12. Formal primaries remain documentary/policy holds pending peer Atlas practice and the participating-pact roster. No party organisation is an office. Extraordinary constitutional bodies do not recur on an invented ordinary schedule.
13. Office existence and country-level dates do not depend on the global ~18-month alert window. Every current family has an upcoming-calendar link, including 2028 and 2029 formulas. Formal convocatoria is pending/not verified; the pack deliberately does not manufacture exact future days.
14. The 223-column map is documentary. Publication, ingest-attempt and successful-release fields are uninstantiated. All approvals are false. No evidence score, source hash, vote, seat, event or relationship is filled merely to make an import pass.
