# Research gaps and era gates — Chile BJ

`research_coverage_complete=false`; `current_office_inventory_complete=true`. These statements describe different coverage dimensions. The current register and all ordinary upcoming families are present even though some historical result work remains.

| Gate | Documentary treatment / remaining work |
|---|---|
| President / ballotage | Constitution 25–29: popular ordinary office; runoff separate; no invented VP office. Extraordinary vacancy/candidate-death formulas are conditional, with no triggered date. Pre-1989 identities and results are not asserted. |
| Cámara electoral eras | SERVEL historical data 1989–2013 belong to binomial-era districts; Law 20840 reforms the system for 2017. Current 155 seats / 28 districts are not backfilled to the 120-seat / 60-district era. Exact historic district boundaries remain in source fields; no guessed merger graph. |
| Senado electoral eras | Former 38 elected-seat structure also had appointed/life-member eras. Those appointed positions are not popular office rows. Expansion was phased: the 2017–2021 transition must not be presented as 50 simultaneously elected seats. Certified 2021 and 2025 elected summaries reconcile to 27+23=50. Next cohorts are 2029 and 2033. |
| Governor popularization | First direct election 15–16 May 2021, runoff 13 June; 16 regional executive offices in current register. Earlier intendentes were appointed; no popular backfill. Presidency-delegate institutions are excluded. |
| CORE selection mode | Direct first poll 17 November 2013, verified in Constitution transitional 26. Before then indirect arrangements require separate sourcing, not popular event generation. Ñuble did not exist as a region in 2013/2017; no extra CORE event is generated there. 2021 CORE poll 21 November; 2024 aligned to municipal/governor cycle. 2021/2024 CORE result totals still untranscribed. |
| Municipal inventory | SERVEL 2026 lists 346 comunas; Law 19726 grouping yields 345 administrations/690 offices. All current municipalities included. Paihuano/Paiguano and La Calera/Calera CUT joins remain spelling-crosswalk holds; the SERVEL current-office rows are retained. |
| Mayor selection eras | Popular separate mayor ballot from 2004 in SERVEL archives. 1992–2000 municipal source labels distinguish elected mayors/concejales within combined ballots; no separate popular mayor event is invented. Historical indirect/threshold selection rules need dated municipal-act analysis before further normalization. |
| Municipal history aliases | Historical CALERA, LLAY-LLAY, MARCHIGUE and AISEN do not silently join different normalized current names. Unlinked events/results retain original territory and source rows. Cabo de Hornos y Antártica and Navarino use the explicitly sourced grouping/rename law. |
| 2021 council workbook warnings | Huasco, Quilpué, Cartagena, Viña del Mar, Rancagua and San Joaquín have explicit source notices saying en revisión. Their observed figures are retained under hold_source_in_review. Rows with a blank candidate label and numeric codes 900/901 are separately typed unlabelled_source_code; numeric votes are preserved without guessing the code meaning. Workbook footer totals are retained in diagnostics and are not counted again. |
| Missing mesa values | Some 2021 mayor/council source vote cells are blank. An aggregate with any missing component has votes=null, a missing-component count, and a separately labelled known_vote_component_sum. That subtotal is not the completed result and blank cells are not zero. |
| 1992 date inconsistency | SERVEL summary workbook gives 28 October 1992 while its process page and Interior circular 3/1992 identify 28 June 1992. Source dates retained; normalized date is null with a named conflict, not an invented correction. |
| 2023 header inconsistency | Some TRICEL Constitutional Council workbook headers say 7 May 2022 although file/year index and SERVEL process page establish 7 May 2023. Raw headers are retained and dated event uses the independent official process evidence. Elected-name/parity completeness remains a hold where summary spelling differs. |
| Constitutional process | Convención Constitucional 2021 and Consejo Constitucional 2023 are distinct historical extraordinary institutions. Reserved indigenous candidatures are within these bodies. The 2020/2022/2023 plebiscites are documentary process context, not offices; plebiscite-result transcription is not included in the elected-office result count. No next extraordinary body is assumed. |
| 2024 results | All 16 governor,16 CORE,345 mayor and 345 council ordinary contest rows recorded. The 11 governor runoff summaries are certified TRICEL. First-round governor downloadable cells on the TRICEL index were empty; official interactive SERVEL links retained. Municipal/CORE/first-round governor totals remain untranscribed, never 0. |
| San Ramón partial repeat | Decree 154/2021 orders 11 July 2021 repeat in named mesas following partial annulment. Original, repeat and final dataset qualification remain separate. No unverified allocation of final votes to the original or repeated subset. A wider nationwide repeat/annulment audit remains open. |
| Primary policy | Statutory twentieth-Sunday formulas for national and local primaries are in the upcoming calendar. Supplied context does not establish peer Atlas primary normalization; no party office or accepted primary-event row is minted. Actual parties/pacts/territories require a later official roster. |
| Early/snap/dissolution | No scheduled future snap or repeat verified. Do not import Bulgaria-style parliamentary contingencies into Chile. Constitution 51 vacancy replacements are not ordinary popular by-elections. Presidential contingency formulas are documented separately. |
| Territorial changes | 2007 regions,2018 Ñuble and comuna creation/status reforms need detailed dated boundary/identity crosswalks before a full longitudinal geographic graph. No guessed successor edges. Current region labels do not replace raw historic labels. |
| Excluded tiers | EP=0: Chile is not an EU member. No MERCOSUR/Andean/regional parliament, appointed cabinet, party organ, provincial elected institution or speculative nested assembly. |
| Alert window | The ~18-month window applies only to global homepage/alerts. 2028 local/regional and 2029 national families remain first-class country-surface upcoming dates. This pack rescinds the empty-stub research filter for Chile; it does not change the live UI. |
| Delivery / approvals | Research files only, applied_changes=0. No repo/importer/SQLite/VPS/UI mutation. Justin approval boxes are unchecked. |
