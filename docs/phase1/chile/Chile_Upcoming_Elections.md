- **President — first round:** Constitution arts 25–26. Four-year term begins 11 March 2026; no exact day represented as officially called.
- **President — conditional second round:** Article 26; two highest candidates. Condition: More than two candidates and none exceeds half of valid votes.
- **Cámara de Diputados:** 155 current seats; arts 26,47,51; do not project old binomial districts.
- **Senado — next renewal cohort:** Eight-year terms, staggered every four years. Certified 2021 sheets cover 27 seats in nine constituencies; 2025 sheets cover 23 seats in seven. The 2025 cohort next renews in 2033 under the same November formula; not all 50 seats in 2029.
- **Gobernadores regionales:** Law 19.175 art 83 + Law 18.695 art 106. The two-day 2024 exception is not carried forward.
- **Governors — conditional second round:** Law 19.175 art 98 bis; the 2024 second ballot covered 11 regions, not all 16. Condition: More than two candidates and none reaches 40% of valid votes.
- **Consejos regionales (CORE):** Direct popular layer; 2021 CORE vote was in November, separately from May governors/municipalities.
- **Alcaldes — 345 municipal administrations:** Popular municipal ballot. No general mayoral runoff is invented; lawful tie or court-repeat paths require their own evidence.
- **Concejales — 345 municipal councils:** All 346 comunas covered; Cabo de Hornos–Antártica shares one administration under Law 19726.
- **Formal national / pact primaries — conditional documentary hold:** Peer Atlas treatment not established from supplied prompts; no primary office or accepted primary event minted. Parliamentary participation is conditional. Condition: Qualifying parties or pacts participate under the primary law.
- **Formal mayor / governor primaries — conditional documentary hold:** No primary contests for CORE or concejales invented; pact/territory roster pending. Condition: Qualifying parties or pacts participate.
- **Constitutional process / any new extraordinary elected body:** The two historic bodies are not successor chambers of Congress. Plebiscites are not offices.
- **Presidential vacancy / candidate-death contingency:** If less than two years remain, Congress selects a replacement; no new popular or vice-presidential office. Article 28 absolute inability of president-elect is another 90-day path.
- **Court annulment / repeat; purported snap or dissolved-Congress path:** No current general congressional dissolution/snap mechanism established. Article 51 vacancies use replacement rules; never manufacture a national snap calendar. San Ramón original and repeat kept separate.
