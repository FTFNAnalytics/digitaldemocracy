# Iceland identity rules — Prompt AV rebuild

1. Current and historical municipal councils are temporal offices. A same-name predecessor is not automatically the same office across a legal merger.
2. A successor edge exists only when a cited official/statistical source identifies the amalgamation; no name-based guessed edges.
3. Cycle universes are validated independently: 74 (2014), 72 (2018), 64 (2022), 61 (2026).
4. Election modes remain proportional-list, unrestricted/personal, or unopposed/no-poll as the source describes them.
5. Missing votes/shares/seats are NULL with an explicit status, never zero.
6. Council-selected chair/executive-manager positions do not create direct-executive offices.
7. The President is a direct national executive; Alþingi is the national legislature; there is no Iceland EP delegation.
8. Landskjörstjórn combined reporting labels are preserved as source views and never used to fabricate a merger date.
9. Every draft tier is 1:1 with an office row and remains `justin_approved=false`.
10. `applied_changes=0`; publication/ingest/SQLite/VPS/UI fields in the 223-column map remain NOT EXECUTED.
