# Territorial counts and Land mechanisms

Snapshot: Destatis GV100AD, 31 August 2026. Municipality counts are territorial counts; they are not the number of direct mayors or distinct council offices. Independent city councils appear once at municipal level. Bremen's two municipal bodies are separately retained; Berlin and Hamburg share the city/Land representative body. SH's 27 small Gemeindeversammlungen are not elected councils.

Lohheide and Osterheide have separate elected inhabitants’ councils despite non-municipal territorial status. The SH direct-mayor count is a verified subset, not a full-mode denominator. Other optional submunicipal bodies remain an open register gate. No national uniformity is assumed for Ämter, Verwaltungsgemeinschaften, Ortschaften or Stadtbezirke.
