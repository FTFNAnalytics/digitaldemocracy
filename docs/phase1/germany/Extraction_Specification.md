# Research extraction specification

The delivered normalized JSONL is a documentary extraction, not an importer or database migration. There are no repository, SQLite, VPS or UI changes.

Destatis GV100AD is decoded as UTF-8 before slicing its 220-character records. Record types distinguish Länder, administrative districts, associations, Kreise, municipalities and gemeindefreie areas. Types 65/66 do not create ordinary municipal councils. The two source-established NI inhabitants’ councils are separately included; other non-municipal areas are not presumed to have elected bodies. Current county councils exclude independent cities already represented at municipal/Land level. The ZIP's explicit Bautzen name correction is retained.

Federal ZIP archives preserve each cycle's own reporting geography. CSV headers determine current versus prior-period columns. Flat files are preferred when the official archive supplies them. Election metrics are matched as metric labels, never by finding the word “Wähler” inside a party name. Thus FREIE WÄHLER and Nichtwähler remain contestant labels. Different vote components stay separate.

Official Land history PDF tables are split into left/right panels, dated and parsed by source page. Metric labels, including Gesamtstimmen, Ausgefallene Stimmen and ballot counts, are not parties. Pre-1990 panels cannot spill into a 1990 event. The contradictory Hamburg heading is resolved by independent official records. Early history is normalized with explicit former-body identities and territorial footprints. Fifteen incompatible seat panels and Bavaria 1950 party vote labels are withheld; retained source values remain reviewable. Checks reconcile comparable numeric observations, not withheld fields.

Bavarian council tables are extracted using the printed table geometry. Regional and Landkreis summary rows are excluded from municipal totals. Weighted figures are retained outside votes. All 2,056 municipal and 71 Kreis council seat totals reconcile. The separate executive workbook supplies dated first/runoff ballots and raw counts. Saxony's workbook retains original code, source status EE/VE, election date, ballot number and reform note.

Hesse rows are chosen by published jurisdiction type: VF/KS for municipalities and LK for Kreise; local WG labels are supplied by Kurzname columns. Lower Saxony's Kreis table is preliminary; its municipality comparison file is only retained because it aggregates municipal elections by Kreis. NRW's blank precinct identifiers receive a source-row locator to prevent collisions, not an invented geographic identifier. SH precinct returns and whole-council seat tables remain separate reporting units; their local party-field dictionaries are context-scoped. Bremen Beirat extraction uses published area totals, without adding their precinct rows again.

Brandenburg’s 413 municipal returns preserve Hirschfeld’s March election and September repeats at Bad Liebenwerda and Cottbus. Rheinland-Pfalz source endpoints are taken from the authority-linked application and its published geographic tree; only root-office totals are normalized. Nested territories and totals/groups are not double-counted. Missing candidate identities quarantine two endpoints. Absent No votes and incomplete seat columns create partial-universe flags, not inferred zero/residual values. Every retained RP endpoint has an extraction disposition.

NRW borough and municipal PDF tables use source-defined zero dashes and distinct 2025/current versus 2020/comparison columns. Munich archive rows are limited to whole-borough totals. Saxon Ortschaft tables use explicit ORS identifiers and consolidated-cycle anchors; source statutory seats and occupied seats stay separate. Federal Convention history covers all 17 conventions and 26 ballots, including the explicit 1989 No option.

Official territorial workbooks contribute 133 change rows. Only 26 explicit whole-unit dissolutions/new formations connecting source-identified historical office codes enter the territorial crosswalk. Partial transfers are retained without office-successor assertions.

No votes are back-solved, no missing transfer papers invented, no elected flags inferred from plurality, no successor edges guessed, and no unsourced exact future polling dates are created. The validator is read-only, standard-library Python and checks the frozen delivery without changing its files.
