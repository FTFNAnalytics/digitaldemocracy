# Identity and result rules

1. The office is the elected body or separately elected executive, not each member, constituency, polling station, reporting total or incumbent. A source reporting-unit row cannot create an office by itself.
2. Current territorial identity uses the retained Destatis AGS/ARS with 2026-08-31 vintage. Historical identities use the election-era code and a historical-only key. Code retirement does not automatically mean legal abolition; effective dates remain null without explicit evidence. The territorial crosswalk records only source-coded whole-unit relations, with office_continuity_asserted=false; it does not silently merge office identities.
3. Bundestag, Bundespräsident and Germany's EP delegation are separate body/office identities. Bundesrat and Chancellor have no popular office rows. Bundespräsident events use a Federal Convention electorate and numbered convention ballots, never a nationwide popular electorate.
4. Bundestag first and second votes are parallel ballot components, not first/second rounds. In 1949 the one-vote regime is preserved. 2025 second-vote coverage and 630-seat rules must not be projected onto overhang/balance-era elections. Published seat counts are evidence, not outputs of an invented algorithm.
5. Original 2021 Bundestag and the 2024 Berlin partial-repeat updated full national result remain distinct event variants. The updated national return is not a new ordinary 2024 Bundestag election. Berlin's 2023 Land repeat is linked only by cycle metadata, without a guessed supersession edge.
6. Land votes differ: Bavaria publishes combined first/second vote totals; Hamburg and Bremen permit multiple votes; BW's 2026 ballot differs from older cycles. A principal-vote column is not universally second votes or one vote per voter.
7. Preserve popular mayor and Landrat ballots only where the Land/local legal class supports them. Honorary does not by itself mean indirect. NI Samtgemeinde member mayors, BW/SH Landräte and city-state executives require their specific exclusions. SH unknown mode is unresolved, not presumed indirect.
8. Berlin/Hamburg city and Land representation is not duplicated as an extra city council. Bremen Land Bürgerschaft and Stadtbürgerschaft have different electoral footing; retain distinct bodies. Independent city councils do not acquire duplicate Kreis councils merely because their AGS occupies the Kreis level.
9. SH Gemeindeversammlungen consist of eligible residents; do not invent elected council seats. Population alone does not establish a new council after the source election vintage.
10. Ordinary local election, runoff, Saxon second ballot, by-election and repeat are source-qualified event variants. A Saxon second ballot is not assumed to be a top-two runoff. Never derive a vote total from the winning percentage.
11. Result identities include unit, event and vote type. Source-local WGR/D slots and party/list strings are not universal parties. No party organisations or speculative cross-election party mappings are created.
12. Results include metrics and seat tables as explicitly labelled rows. Sum only comparable contestant rows in one reporting universe. Do not add nationwide totals to their Länder/constituencies or precincts, or list totals to candidate components.
13. Weighted Bavarian figures are stored as weighted_votes_raw and votes=null. Hesse raw and weighted figures are distinct. Blank, X or unavailable detail is not converted to zero. NRW’s published legend defines a dash as exact zero; that source-specific exception is recorded. Other unqualified dashes remain null.
14. NI unoccupied seats are preserved separately from published occupied seats. Federal historic seat tables retain their published Berlin-delegate definition. Do not claim a current parliament size from a historical denominator.
15. Published official final, preliminary, parliamentary record, Endergebnis without explicit certification, and unresolved download/conflict statuses remain distinct. Gazette certification, court annulment and republication require their own evidence.
16. West German / West Berlin election footprints before reunification are historical. No GDR body is a current Federal Republic office. Reunification and territorial reforms are named gates, not inferred successor links.
17. Exact source member, row, sheet, page or whole-table scope is retained. Reviewers must use raw files where a label is abbreviated or unresolved. The Hamburg heading is resolved with two independent official sources while the original remains retained. Berlin 2026 exports and two unidentified RP candidate returns remain quarantined. Fifteen conflicting Land seat panels and Bavaria 1950 party votes are withheld from numeric normalization.
18. Current existence and history are never filtered by the 18-month upcoming-alert window. Calendar precision is preserved; a known year is not padded into an exact polling day.
19. Draft tiers are jurisdiction proposals only. Every current and historical office gets exactly one; no Justin approval, publication or production status is implied.
