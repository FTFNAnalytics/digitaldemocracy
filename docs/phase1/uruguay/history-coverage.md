National archives: 1954,1962,1966,1971,1984,1989,1999,2004,2009,2014,2019,2024; 1994 contemporaneous IPU context and lower-house seats with numerical holds. Actual runoffs: 1999,2009,2014,2019,2024.1952 era events belong to the collegiate executive, not invented presidential contests.1958 and older full series remain open.

Departmental archives: 1954,1962,1966,1971,1984,1989,2000,2005,2010,2015,2020,2025.1954/1962 unambiguous Junta seat distributions are retained but ambiguous vote grids are held. Selected historical Intendente winner identities have no fabricated personal votes.2010 Florida’s overwritten winner text is held. Departmental 1994 returns were not normalized from national data.

Municipal histories: 89 observed units in 2010,112 in 2015,125 in 2020,136 in 2025. Party vote columns/groups are transcribed for every observed unit, preserving one blank 2015 cell. All 32 Canelones 2025 party vectors match the signed proclamation; their allocated party seats are summed from explicit list awards. No seat-allocation algorithm is used to fill other untranscribed totals.125 municipal 2020 holder identities come from the official OPP post-election roster.53 municipal 2025 alcalde identities and all 19 Intendente winner identities are normalized from proclamations; the remaining 83 alcalde identities and many candidate/seat details are still in retained PDFs.

Publication status is record-specific: official archive summary, official aggregate return, post-election holder roster, signed certified proclamation, or OCR-assisted draft transcription. A signed source does not mean every extraction was manually certified. Retained numeric source contradictions are explained in research-gaps.md. Shares remain null; turnout denominators are not inferred from inconsistent processing-stage columns. No deferred, unheld, party-primary or referendum result rows are invented.
