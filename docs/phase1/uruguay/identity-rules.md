# Uruguay BF — Identity and result rules

1. `country_id=uruguay`, `country_code=UY`; República Oriental del Uruguay only. No regional-parliament, EP, cabinet or party-organ offices.
2. One President and one Vice President office; one joint popular ticket. Two national chamber offices; no third duplicated “General Assembly” popular office. The VP’s Senate role is ex officio.
3. A department contains distinct elected Intendente and Junta Departamental offices. The 1952-era Concejo Departamental is a separate source-identified historical collective executive, not a second current Intendente.
4. Current municipios come from signed Circular 12208. IDs are pack-local, based on the Corte department abbreviation and municipal letter; `NY` encodes source `Ñ` only in an ID. They are not claimed to be ISO or permanent statutory identifiers. Parent geography expresses nesting, never succession.
5. A municipal council office contains five elected seats. Its alcalde role is an additional office-role row representing a seat already included in the council. There is no invented separate executive ballot and no sixth member.
6. Current municipio names and source spelling variants are preserved. Barros Blanco/Barros Blancos and Miguez/Migues do not create extra offices. Repeated names in different departments, including La Paz, Quebracho and Cerro Chato, remain separate because their official department and electoral-letter keys differ.
7. The 2010 Cap. Juan Artigas source label is an obsolete city-name reference supported by Law 18.136 and the 2010 municipal law. It does not establish a municipality before 2010. Florida 2015 “Fray Bentos” is a source-summary typo reconciled against detailed Fray Marcos returns; no Florida Fray Bentos body is created.
8. Elected Juntas Locales Autónomas y Electivas of Río Branco, Bella Unión and San Carlos remain historical-only rows. Same place name does not establish exact boundary continuity or a successor edge. All predecessor/successor fields remain null.
9. National co-elections and municipal council/alcalde roles share `ballot_group_id`. A VP or alcalde event may reference its paired ballot event. Do not add common national lema totals across President, Senate and Representatives, or party ballots to candidate votes.
10. Historic party labels are source-local. P.D.C., Frente Amplio and predecessor alliances are not assigned a guessed enduring party identity. “Others” in the 1994 IPU report stays an aggregate, not an invented party.
11. A source’s explicit zero is retained; missing, blank, untranscribed, inapplicable and unresolved values remain null. All shares are null unless actually transcribed with a denominator; none are manufactured here. Party-table zero columns do not establish that candidates stood in that unit.
12. Event dates are polling dates, separate from proclamations, assumption of office, ties, substitutions and publication dates. The 2020 deferred May schedule has no results. San Bautista’s 2025 draw is a proceeding, not another election.
13. 1918/1934/1942/1952/1967/1997 constitutional gates prevent back-projecting current rules, Senate mandates, popular-executive identities or calendars. No invented contests during the military interruption. Independence/colonial eras are not treated as today’s office series.
14. Current register completeness does not imply complete historical candidate/seat transcription. Dates and result numbers come from retained cited sources, including explicitly identified conflicts and holds. No inferred winner solely from a maximum party vote.
15. Upcoming rows use a legal formula and next year; a formal convocatoria is pending in the retrieved material. All current offices remain in the country calendar regardless of the 18-month global-alert horizon. Historical-only offices receive no upcoming calendar entry.
16. No normalization rule performs database, importer, repository, VPS, UI or publication writes. Every tier is a draft institutional-level classification, not a swing score. All Justin approval fields remain false.
