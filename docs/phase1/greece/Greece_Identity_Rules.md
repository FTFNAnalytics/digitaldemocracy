# Greece identity and election rules

1. Offices are governmental bodies or executive roles, not parties, people, parliamentary seats or polling stations. Namespace `greece-ypes-research-v2` is internal research identity. It is not asserted to be an official national identifier.
2. `GR-PARL`, `GR-PRES` and `GR-EP` identify Parliament, the indirect presidency and the Greek EP delegation. `GR-R-##-C/G` identifies each regional council/governor pair; `GR-M-####-C/M` identifies each municipal council/mayor pair. Source codes retain their separate upstream meaning.
3. Council and direct executive offices are distinct even when elected on the same joint ticket. The winning head of that ticket is popularly selected. Mid-term vacancies or council-selected replacements do not create a fictitious popular election.
4. President is an indirect office with ballot basis `electors`; the electors are MPs. Keep each evidenced ballot. No mass-electorate share or turnout exists in these observations. The 2014 unsuccessful ballots and February 2015 election are grouped as a constitutional sequence across dissolution/new Parliament, not a popular two-round race.
5. Constitutional article 32, amended in 2019, is a named gate. Current thresholds are two ballots requiring two-thirds, a third requiring three-fifths, then an absolute majority, then relative majority. Earlier dissolution-linked procedures are retained in their period; current rules are not projected backward.
6. Kallikratis (Law 3852/2010) is the regional/municipal architecture gate. The 2010 election selected authorities for the new 2011 arrangement. Contemporary archive registers supply the actual election geography.
7. Kleisthenis (Law 4555/2018), the 2019 territorial reforms identified in Ministry circular 83/20-08-2019, and the 2021/2023 electoral framework are separate gates. The five municipalities abolished on 31 August 2019 are historical; twelve new municipalities operated from 1 September 2019. Their 2019 elections precede the administrative start date and are valid records for the incoming offices.
8. Reused source codes 9069, 9118, 9120, 9261 and 9264 are versioned with `PRE2019` for the old authority. Their new names/boundaries never overwrite the old offices. Seven additional source codes occur only in post-reform registers. The circular is retained, but no successor graph is guessed or asserted.
9. The March 2026 municipality reconciliation normalizes accents, case and punctuation only. Three documented spelling aliases are explicit. The two Ηρακλείου municipalities are disambiguated by region/nomos and distinct electoral and TPD codes, not merged by name.
10. Source regional, regional-district, municipality and municipal-district codes are separate namespaces. A municipal district displayed in an election portal is not automatically a standalone elected municipality office. Appointed decentralized administrations, cabinets, party organizations and unestablished community offices are excluded.
11. An event is one office-cycle, with separate proceedings where evidenced. Council/executive events share a `shared_ballot_id`. A result has stable event/proceeding/observation identity; labels are not identity keys. No date-only cross-country or cross-office merge is allowed.
12. Local first-round observations support two projections. Council result rows have no first-round proceeding link because their seats describe the complete published contest allocation; executive rows link to first round/runoff. A runoff is created only when the authority source reports actual second-round returns. Unused B fields containing placeholders do not create a contest.
13. Source seat formulas differ: 2010/2014 use AEdres+BEdres, 2019 uses AEdres, and 2023 uses BEdres when positive, otherwise AEdres. The retained portal rendering code is interpretive evidence. Direct/indirect executive rows never receive council-seat totals.
14. `WIN_ID=0` is not a candidate code. Missing winners remain null, even where a rank or tie might tempt a guess. Incomplete allocations are retained with their source hold rather than rescaled to council size.
15. National lists include explicit source aggregate/zero categories where published; these are tagged rather than fabricated as canonical parties. Party mappings and cross-cycle person IDs remain unassigned. Do not fuzzy-merge local slates or coalitions.
16. Percentages are percent units, 0–100. Exact votes are never reconstructed from rounded percentages. Blank, absent, zero and not-applicable are distinct states. An aggregate of unknown votes is not a zero-vote result.
17. EP constitutive-session composition is labelled as such; the 1979–1984 outgoing composition is not imported as the 1981 election result. Earlier event dates stay at year precision when the selected evidence supplies only the year.
18. Official publication is not the same as certified legal finality. Source status, time and reporting denominator are preserved. Recounts, annulments or court replacements require their own evidence and are never synthesized.
19. The 18-month window applies only to upcoming alerts. Every included current office and historical record remains in this pack even with no nearby election. No inferred future ordinary date is presented as scheduled.
20. Draft classification is 1:1 for all current and historical offices. Review status remains `needs_review`, approvals are unassigned, and no operational release or import state is minted.
