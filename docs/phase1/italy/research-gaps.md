# Italy — research gaps and exclusions

No item below is an approval. Missing values and uncollected cycles are not zero.

## IT-G01 — coverage_boundary

Status: open.

All 7,894 current comuni, their councils and popularly elected mayors, the 20 regional councils, evidenced regional direct executives, the autonomous provinces, national offices and EP delegation are enumerated. Submunicipal coverage is limited to the five source-established Firenze quartieri. Historical returns are substantial but not exhaustive. Current incumbency, commissions and actual office vacancy are not asserted.

Next evidence needed: Complete the other elected circoscrizioni/municipi classes and historical cycles before claiming full requested research coverage.

## IT-G02 — presidential_indirect_election

Status: mechanism_resolved_history_bounded.

Constitution arts 83–85: Parliament in joint session plus regional delegates; Valle d’Aosta has one delegate rather than three. Two-thirds of the assembly for the first three ballots, absolute majority thereafter. Thirteen Quirinale winning-ballot tables from 1948–2022 are retained; preceding ballots are not reconstructed. No national popular votes, popular turnout or first-preference universe exists.

Next evidence needed: Collect the other actual parliamentary ballot records if complete ballot-by-ballot history is required; keep the 1946 provisional head of state distinct.

## IT-G03 — Camera_Senato_law_vintages

Status: version_gate.

The 1993 mixed system, 2005 list/bonus law, 2017 mixed law and 2020 constitutional reduction are separate legal vintages. The 2022 official domestic returns are normalized at their original municipal/constituency grain. The mixed vote links list and single-member candidates; it is not a German-style independently cast first-/second-vote universe. Current constitutional sizes are 400 deputies and 200 elected senators; unelected life membership is distinct.

Next evidence needed: Collect earlier national cycles, overseas returns, parliamentary validation and the law effective for each cycle. Proposals or term length never authorize fabricated seat arithmetic or dates.

## IT-G04 — regional_president_selection

Status: mechanism_gate.

Eighteen regional presidents are registered as direct offices. Valle d’Aosta and Trentino-Alto Adige presidents are selected through their councils and have no invented popular executive contest. The TAA regional council consists of the two elected provincial councils; its current regional office must not generate a separate regional ballot.

Next evidence needed: Attach cycle-specific special-statute and ordinary-region electoral laws to future detailed returns. Only Lombardia and Lazio 2023 regional vote tables are normalized here.

## IT-G05 — Delrio_provincial_metropolitan

Status: explicit_exclusion_with_exception.

Ordinary provincial presidents/councils are second-degree under Law 56/2014; metropolitan councils are second-degree and the metropolitan mayor is ordinarily the capital’s mayor ex officio. They are dispositions, not invented popular offices. Sicily and Sardinia official 2025 notices also evidence indirect elections. The two autonomous provinces remain included under their own rules.

Next evidence needed: Retain pre-Delrio popular provincial history only from dated official returns; it is not normalized here. Do not apply an ordinary provincial rule to special-statute arrangements.

## IT-G06 — FVG_2026_provinces

Status: statutory_transition.

Friuli Venezia Giulia LR 7 of 14 July 2026 establishes four provinces with direct councils and presidents. Eight offices are separately marked statutory_pending_first_election. The statutory text ties first elections to completion of the constitutive process and provides for transfer of functions from 1 January 2027. No election date or past vote is invented.

Next evidence needed: Confirm the implementing acts, actual first election call and operational start before moving these rows into the current elected register.

## IT-G07 — municipal_mayor_and_runoff_modes

Status: mechanism_resolved_rules_versioned.

The ordinary TUEL distinction around 15,000 inhabitants is not a universal special-region formula. TAA, Valle d’Aosta, FVG and Sicily require their own law vintage. Valle d’Aosta also directly elects 74 deputy mayors jointly with mayors, without a second vote universe.

Next evidence needed: Verify population reference date, candidate/list linkage, quorum, thresholds, ties and runoff provisions for each contest. Do not infer a runoff from a vote percentage or make every single-candidate result unanimous.
