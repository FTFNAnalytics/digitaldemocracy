# Romania acceptance examples
+
+1. Alba county council and its president are separate offices.
+2. Alba Iulia council and mayor are separate offices.
+3. Bucharest General Council is not duplicated as an ordinary local council.
+4. Bucharest general mayor is distinct from all six sector mayors.
+5. Sector 1 council and mayor use separate office IDs.
+6. A commune remains in scope even without an upcoming date inside 18 months.
+7. A 2016 county-president direct event is rejected because selection was by council.
+8. 2020 and 2024 county-president direct events are retained.
+9. The 2024 presidential first round is marked annulled.
+10. The 2025 presidential runoff is round 2, not a continuation of the annulled 2024 event.
+11. A missing local result produces no numeric row, not a zero-vote row.
+12. EP 2024 seats sum to 33; party votes/shares remain distinct from EP political-group membership.
+13. Camera Deputaților and Senat results never share an event ID.
+14. A later municipality rename preserves SIRUTA identity only after official reconciliation.
+15. A split/merger gets no predecessor/successor edge without an official instrument.
+16. Appointed prefects are rejected from the office register.
+17. Prime Minister/cabinet are rejected as separately popular offices.
+18. AEP preliminary and final files cannot be silently mixed; certification status travels with the event/result.
+19. Tier classification cannot be inferred from calendar timing.
+20. Unchecked Justin boxes and `draft_for_human_review` fail closed for publication.
