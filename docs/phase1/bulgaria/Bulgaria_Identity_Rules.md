# Bulgaria — identity and evidence rules

1. Keep all 3,597 P office IDs byte-for-byte, including all 530 accepted municipality-wide IDs and 3,067 holds. P geography keys, selected event IDs, history keys, result IDs and retained observation bindings are unchanged. The pinned repository is main commit `c8c3eb9f730e04ecf9b58b7038897c47162e4139`; original payload SHA-256 is `0b6b2c05dd8906f7e7a19927e847d4bc0aa83da8769e70ebbef012b13d0d287e`.
2. Do not make identity from display name alone. P already identified 272 ambiguous name/type groups covering 630 rows. Its office-keyed geography exception remains: no prefix-derived parent, arbitrary redirect, or name-based merger. Preserve Unicode spelling in raw labels; no transliteration-based matching.
3. BI's four new IDs are explicit documentary identifiers: `BG-NATIONAL-ASSEMBLY`, `BG-PRESIDENT-JOINT-TICKET`, `BG-EUROPEAN-PARLIAMENT`, `BG-GRAND-NATIONAL-ASSEMBLY-1990`. They are not government-issued codes or approved live Atlas IDs. No extra office per seat, constituency, polling station, party, runoff, or current incumbent is created.
4. A new historical event's key uses exact office ID, source-supported polling date, and round/kind. Two contests on the same day remain separate through office identity. Two early elections in a year remain separate through their full dates. President/VP is one joint ballot family; the two rounds have distinct event IDs. The 1990 combined allocation explicitly retains both polling dates.
5. BI result IDs append the encounter order within that exact event. This order is frozen with the source snapshots; any refresh must preserve a semantic crosswalk before changing order. Ballot number alone is insufficient: independent candidates can repeat a ballot number across districts. Source label, event, locator and row kind remain attached.
6. Source correction is distinct from a new polling event. The court-corrected October 2024 allocation remains attached to the 27 October event. An original pre-correction allocation was not fully transcribed here and must not be guessed. Annulled, repeat and replacement ballots require separate source decisions and dates before new event IDs are added.
7. `data/Successor_Links.json` is empty. Absence from a later roster is not a deletion. A settlement rename is not automatically an office replacement. The held Stefan Stambolovo/Gradina observation retains one official settlement code but has no fabricated office successor link.
8. Preserve source numbers and zero/missing distinctions. Numeric null means untranscribed/unreported, never zero. Percentages stay in published units and denominators; no reconstructed votes. “Other parties” stays an aggregate; “none of the above” stays a ballot option, not an invented party. Invalid ballots are not seats. Seat totals are only checked for sufficiently complete source allocations.
9. Baseline P and added BI event formats are deliberately separate. P's cycle/date convention is not rewritten to simulate fully transcribed rounds. All 7,746 first-round and 2,591 unresolved observations remain retained input; no fabricated certification follows from their existence.
10. Country calendar IDs own date claims independently of historical result IDs. Called exact days, conditional runoffs, year formulas, and research holds are different states. No day or month is supplied for a year-only claim. No prospective vote or seat result is invented. Country visibility is independent of the global alert window.
11. Every saved source has either an exact local snapshot hash or a clearly described inherited citation without newly fetched original bytes. Search/indexed extraction hashes are not original webpage/PDF hashes. Original payload/member hashes remain recoverable from the included archive and inventory. A successful checksum proves byte integrity, not the truth or certification of a source claim.
12. The validator is a read-only audit of this directory. It does not read/write a database, contact a VPS, change a UI/repository, or implement an importer. All BI approval booleans are false and all new Justin boxes remain unchecked. Original P approval is historical provenance, not a new approval.
