## Interpretation rules

- The eight parliamentary elections from April 2021 through April 2026 remain eight events; 2021 has three and 2024 has two. The October 2024 court-corrected table is a revision of the 27 October event, not a new polling event in March 2025.
- The 1990 Grand National Assembly result is a combined allocation across 10 and 17 June. The two polling dates are retained on one combined-results election event; it is not represented as two separate 400-seat allocations.
- The 2009 National Assembly election used 209 proportional and 31 majority seats in the retrieved IPU table. The later February 2010 composition column is retained as context, not substituted for the initial election allocation; court action and party changes require separate provenance.
- Modern party-list Assembly systems and the 1990/2009 mixed systems must be dated by era. Where a detailed electoral-system rule was not transcribed, the event's field is null rather than extrapolated from today's law.
- President/VP rounds for 2006, 2011, 2016 and 2021 are separate. The 1992, 1996 and 2001 cycles remain a named archival-transcription gap, not zero-vote events. The transition's indirectly chosen presidency is not minted as a popular contest.
- EP contest years are 2007, 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024. Bulgaria's first election followed accession in 2007; the downloaded 2004–2009 outgoing-composition page is not a 2004 Bulgarian election. The 2007 delegation was 18; the 2009 initial allocation and 2014/2019/2024 election tables here total 17. Later treaty/composition changes are not separate elections. Losing EP lists grouped as “Other parties” remain an aggregate, with no synthetic party identities.
- P preserves 587 percentage-only rows across 221 events. EP 2009–2024 summaries also retain votes as null. No votes are derived from percentages; no missing seat or vote is changed to zero. Presidential table columns headed invalid votes are not seat allocations.
- Official publication, final certification, a court correction, and complete transcription are different properties. No generic “certified” label is applied to the whole pack; P's republication caveats remain intact.
