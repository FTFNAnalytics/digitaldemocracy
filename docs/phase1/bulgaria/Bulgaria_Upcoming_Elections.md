The President is popularly elected on a joint ticket with the Vice-President. This pack counts one joint contest family; it does not claim that the Vice-President is appointed, nor invent two independent ballots. Municipal mayors are popular contests in the sourced P era/current Election Code. Municipal councils have no mayor-style runoff.

A derived year is not a scheduled day. Future year/formula cards retain null day/month fields. The called Polski Trambesh by-election does not replace the 2027 ordinary family or generate a second mayor office. The presidential and mayoral contests on 25 October remain distinct proceedings.

The Bulgarian text of Article 64(3) says **before** expiry. The Court's English rendering retrieved in this pass says after; the Bulgarian text governs this calendar. No expiry anniversary is converted into a precise polling day. The Article 99 contingency is retained without assuming that a snap election automatically entails a dissolution under every constitutional era.

CIK Decision 126-MI also identifies 13 village-mayor by-elections on 18 October 2026, with possible 25 October runoffs. Those are **submunicipal_scope holds**.
