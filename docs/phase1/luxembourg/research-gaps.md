# Research gaps and exclusions

## LU-G01 — Grand Duke

**resolved_exclusion**. Hereditary constitutional head of state. No popular office/event/result created. See official Grand Duke source.

## LU-G02 — Mayoral selection

**resolved_exclusion**. Mayor appointed by Grand Duke on presentation of council majority; college derives from elected council. No separate popular mayoral or executive contest.

## LU-G03 — Communal mergers

**bounded_gap**. 28 explicit predecessor edges retained. Exact legal effective days for 2011-law and Wiltz mergers not independently projected; portal establishes names and law dates. Older pre-2005 council universes not exhaustively reconstructed.

## LU-G04 — Supplementary elections / uncontested vacancies

**open**. All four ordinary-cycle archives extracted; by-elections filling vacancies and subsequent changes are not an exhaustive all-years series. In particular Consdorf/Nommern/Schieren/Weiler-la-Tour 2011, Bech 2017, Nommern 2023 require follow-up beyond the initial return. No invented later winners.

## LU-G05 — Certification

**open**. Most source vectors are official-publisher unofficial snapshots; complete reporting does not make them certified. Certified parliamentary PDFs (1994 retained) and later judgments/corrections can differ; do not replace source status.

## LU-G06 — EP

**bounded_gap**. 1994 vote detail is communal reporting-unit data with separate EP constitutive seats. 1999–2024 national party/candidate vectors retained. Pre-1994 elections outside the extracted portal series; not claimed exhaustive since 1979.

## LU-G07 — Parliament

**bounded_gap**. 1994 commune candidate/list returns retained, with certified district PDFs as evidence; certified seat-vector transcription is not complete. 1999–2023 four-constituency party/candidate returns. Pre-1994 election history not claimed complete.

## LU-G08 — Dates

**bounded_gap**. Older national/EP HTML archive rows use source-supported election year when an exact day was not separately bound. Do not auto-fill Jan 1.

## LU-G09 — Berdorf

**resolved_with_capture_limit**. 2023 postponed poll held October 8. Official commune proclamation has 15 candidates and 9 elected; web PDF extraction and visual verification retained. Original PDF direct download returned 403; hash applies to extraction only.

## LU-G10 — Raw totals inconsistencies

**review**. Legacy 2005 HTML can publish zero blank/invalid ballots despite a gap between ballot and valid totals. Preserve raw, do not repair by subtraction or treat as certified.

## LU-G11 — 1994 EP archive defect

Grevenmacher HTML omits the LSAP block, including candidates. No residual vote total is invented. Geographic sums cannot be promoted to a complete certified national result.
